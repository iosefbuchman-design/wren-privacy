Wren: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy describes the Wren speaking-practice app ("the app").

Short version

The app works without an account, shows no ads, has no analytics and does not track you. Your recordings and your text are processed on your device. An account is optional and only keeps a copy of your progress.

What stays on your device

  • Your recordings are analysed on the device. They are kept only if you choose to keep them, and you can delete them at any time.
  • Without an account, your practice history, saved words, plan and settings are stored on the device only. Deleting the app, or using "delete my data" in Settings, removes them.

Optional account

If you create an account (email and password, Google or Apple), we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign you in and to keep one copy of your progress: practice history (including the text of your spoken answers, as recognised on your device), saved words, plan and settings. Recordings and videos are never uploaded. We store your email address and an account ID for this. Firebase is contacted only after you open the sign-in page or once you are signed in. You can delete the account and its copy in the app at any time (Profile → delete account).

Camera and microphone

The microphone is used only while you record an answer. The camera is used only in the optional video mirror (if it is available in your version), to show where you looked away or smiled. Both are analysed on the device; the recording is not kept after you leave the screen and is never sent anywhere.

Videos from YouTube and TikTok

Some lessons show short clips from official YouTube and TikTok accounts in their own players. These players and their preview pictures are loaded only after you allow them in the app. Once loaded, YouTube or TikTok may set their own cookies and see your network address, under their own privacy policies. You can withdraw this in Settings → External videos.

What is downloaded from the internet

  • The speech-recognition model (about 140 MB) is downloaded once from huggingface.co and cdn.jsdelivr.net. These services can see that your device's network address requested the file. No audio and no text are sent in that request.

The one feature that sends your voice

The optional voice conversation with the mentor, if it is available in your version, sends your voice to a speech service (Google) for the time of the call. The app does not store that audio. You are asked before it starts. If you never use it, nothing is sent.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifier, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, no location, no contacts. We do not sell or share personal data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/iosefbuchman-design/wren-privacy/issues.
